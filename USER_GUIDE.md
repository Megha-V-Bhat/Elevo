# Elevo user guide

## Everyone
- **Sign in** with the email your HOD invited. Forgot your password? Use "Forgot password?" on the sign-in page.
- **Navigate** with the dock at the bottom of the screen (or the tab bar and **+** button on phones). Press **Ctrl K** (⌘K on Mac) or **/** to search pages and people.
- **Notifications** appear on the bell. Open, mark done, or snooze them for a day. Choose email preferences in **Account**.

## Students (mentees)
1. **Set up your profile** the first time you sign in. It saves as you go, so you can leave and come back.
2. **Academics:** add a semester. Subjects are pre-filled from your department; type marks or paste them from Excel. On a phone you get one subject per screen. **Submit for verification** when the semester is complete. Spot a mistake after verification? Use **Request correction**.
3. **Insights:** your growth rings, SGPA trend, MSE vs SEE, and strengths and focus areas. Tap **Why am I seeing this?** for the evidence. You're only ever compared with your own past.
4. **Weekly Pulse:** three questions, about 30 seconds. Your mentor sees your mood and workload, and your note only if you choose to share it. Skipping a week is fine.
5. **Ask my mentor:** a private question with up to 3 attachments. Mark it resolved when you're sorted.
6. **Grade Goal Planner:** set a CGPA target, or pick a subject and grade to see the minimum SEE marks you need, with the full working shown. Share a plan with your mentor so they can turn it into a goal.
7. **Mentoring, Resources, Sessions, Journey:** feedback and goals (tick off steps and add reflections), study material from your mentor, upcoming sessions (add them to your calendar), and your whole journey as stepping stones.

## Mentors
1. **Home** shows who needs attention, with reasons (rule-based flags, plus Pulse signals marked "self-reported"), plus unanswered questions, pending verifications and your agenda.
2. **Mentees → a student:** tabs for overview, academics, insights, mentoring, resources, sessions, journey, Pulse, Ask, shared plans and the PDF report.
3. **Verify** submitted semesters in bulk, return them with a note, and approve or decline corrections (side-by-side diff).
4. **Inbox:** reply, attach one of your resources, **Convert to recommendation**, or **Suggest a session**.
5. **Recommendations / Resources / Sessions:** write shared items or private notes (only "Shared" + "Include in report" items reach the PDF). Upload files or links for one mentee or all of them. Schedule, complete (action items become trackable points) or cancel sessions.
6. **Reports:** preview or download any mentee's PDF, and export your mentee list.

## HODs
1. **Home:** mentoring coverage, students, mentors, unassigned and flagged counts, a mentor workload table (Ask and Pulse appear only as aggregates), SGPA by section, intervention and incomplete-profile lists.
2. **Assignments:** drag students between mentors (or select several and use **Reassign to…**). Confirm, then **Undo** within 10 seconds if needed. Keyboard: focus a card, press Space, use the arrow keys, press Space again. There are also Table, Mentor → mentees and History views.
3. **Students / Mentors:** search, filter, sort and save views. Finalize verified semesters and see a record's full change history.
4. **Users & roles:** invite, change roles, suspend or reactivate, and resend invites. Elevo always keeps at least one active HOD.
5. **Imports:** download a template, upload, review every row (errors and duplicates flagged), choose to skip or update duplicates, then import and download the error report.
6. **Exports:** department summary, academic summary, mentor workload, assignments and interventions as Excel, CSV or PDF. Pulse, questions and plans are never exportable.
7. **Settings:** grading scheme with live preview (each save is a new version), thresholds, sections and programme credits, optional profile fields, and the Pulse week start.
8. **Audit history:** who changed what, with before/after.

1. HOD → **Assignments**: drag Nisha Kamath onto Dr. Iyer, confirm, show the Undo toast.
2. Student (Ananya) → **Pulse**: check in as "Good" and "Heavy" and share a note. Then **Ask**: "How should I revise normalisation?" Then **Planner**: an A in DBMS needs X/100 in SEE (open "How is this calculated?"), and share a CGPA target.
3. Mentor (Dr. Iyer) → **Home**: Needs attention shows the self-reported Pulse chip. Open the student's **Pulse** tab and reply with a recommendation. In the **Inbox**, convert the question into a recommendation. On the **Plans** tab, turn the shared target into a goal.
4. Mentor → **Download Student Report**: a clean PDF with verified records only.
